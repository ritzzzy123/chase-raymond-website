Before You List

Six things that actually matter

Price it right from day one

Overpricing costs you more than underpricing. Homes that sit too long start to look like something's wrong.

Small fixes beat big renovations

Decluttering, paint, and minor repairs almost always outperform expensive reno dollar for dollar.

Professional photos aren't optional

Most buyers decide whether to book a showing based on photos alone.

Timing affects your sale

London's market has seasonal patterns, I'll tell you honestly if now's the moment.

Know your closing costs up front

Legal fees, commission, and adjustments add up. No surprises if we plan for them early.

Be flexible with showings

More access for buyers generally means more offers, and more leverage for you.
