Before You Start Looking

Six things that actually matter

Get pre-approved before you fall in love with a place

A pre-approval tells you your real budget and makes your offer stronger when it counts.

Don't skip the home inspection

It's the cheapest insurance you'll ever buy on the biggest purchase you'll make.

Budget for closing costs

Plan for roughly 1.5 to 4 percent of the purchase price beyond your down payment.

Know the London market cycle

Timing affects your negotiating power. I'll give it to you straight.

Have your down payment sourced

Lenders want a paper trail. Sort this early so it doesn't slow down your offer.

Work with someone who'll tell you the truth

Not every listing is a good fit, even if it looks great in photos.
