The short version

London, Ontario REALTOR® who tells it straight.

I found real estate through a high school co-op placement and never really looked back. My dad owns a car dealership and always pushed sales, just not his sales, he told me to go find my own lane instead of following him into cars. Real estate turned out to be it. I've been licensed since 2024, so I won't pretend I've got decades behind me, what I've got instead is a full-time obsession with getting better at this and zero patience for doing it the way it's always been done.

Real estate hasn't changed much in thirty years while every other industry has. That's the part that gets me, it's still a client-first business and it deserves agents who actually treat it that way instead of running the same playbook their broker taught them in 2003. That's the whole idea behind Chasing Homes in London (yes, it's a play on my name), being upfront about pricing and timelines and actually showing up on social media instead of hiding behind a business card. Outside of real estate I coach with the London Jr. Knights, work with a goaltending school on the side, and spend most of my downtime running, training, or out at Rondeau Bay fishing and boating. If you want a realtor who treats this like a career and not a script, that's me.
